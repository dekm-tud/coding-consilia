Ask a question in any language. A language model of your choice (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or another compatible service) searches the portal and answers with references to the consilia. Every step is shown: what the model plans, which search it runs, what came back. It runs with your own API key, which stays in this browser; usage is billed to your account with that provider.

Semantic search model (optional)

With a passage-embedding model the assistant can also search by meaning (tool search_semantic) and find similar consilia. The model runs in your browser; it is downloaded once on request and then kept by the browser. Without it the assistant searches by words and by the annotation layers.
